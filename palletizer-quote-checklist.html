How to compare robotic palletizer quotes

Eight scope checks to help purchasing and project teams ask better questions before selecting an integrator.

Try the free comparison demo

Start with a common scope

Send each supplier the same product mix, throughput profile, pallet patterns, available footprint, utilities and acceptance expectations. Record what is unknown. A low headline price can reflect a different scope, so request an itemized list of inclusions, exclusions and assumptions.

Eight checks for every proposal

  1. Product and throughput: Are the range of box sizes, weights, condition, incoming orientation, average and peak rate and required run duration stated? Ask how each rate will be demonstrated.
  2. Robot and end-of-arm tooling: Which robot, controller, software licences, gripper, changeover equipment, wear parts and product trials are included? Check who provides samples.
  3. Infeed and pallet handling: Are conveyors, accumulation, pallet dispensing, pallet exchange, discharge and upstream/downstream interfaces included? Identify where each supplier's boundary ends.
  4. Safety: Ask who is responsible for risk assessment, safeguarding, safety controls and validation of the complete installation. Ensure the applicable requirements are confirmed by a qualified team.
  5. Site and utilities: Identify electrical power, compressed air, network connections, floor preparation, unloading and any site work outside the offer.
  6. Installation and acceptance: Separate freight, installation, commissioning, travel and operator training. Agree FAT and SAT conditions, representative products, test duration and acceptance criteria in writing.
  7. Support and lifecycle: Compare warranty start and coverage, remote support, response commitments, training, documentation and recommended spares. Ask which costs recur.
  8. Commercial assumptions: Align currency, tax treatment, delivery terms, payment milestones, validity, lead time and change-order process before comparing totals.

A simple like-for-like example

Supplier A quotes €125,000 with guarding included. Supplier B quotes €112,000 but excludes guarding. If the buyer independently estimates guarding at €18,000, the planning comparison becomes €125,000 versus €130,000, assuming every other item has been reconciled. These figures are fictional; an estimate is not a supplier commitment. If scope or allowances are unknown, keep the comparison open until they are clarified.
